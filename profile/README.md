Our mission is to help open-source AI flourish: advancing powerful, freely available models, affordable compute, and efficient tools that run on everyday hardware. We champion the freedom to research, build, and use AI so its benefits remain accessible to everyone, beyond the control of any single company or government.

We believe powerful AI should be something people can understand, run, modify, build upon, and share.

## Starting small, building in the open

CommonFIRE is an early-stage initiative. Our starting point is an open discussion of priorities and one concrete project that improves access to capable AI. Governance, funding, and initial project scope are still being developed; these goals are aspirations, not existing programs.
