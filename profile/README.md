## Capable AI. Shared knowledge. More people able to build.

**CommonFIRE — Free Intelligence, Research and Evolution** is an early-stage open-source initiative working toward a simple goal: help people understand, run, modify, and build upon capable AI without needing a large budget or a single provider's permission.

Access takes more than downloadable weights. It takes useful software, clear documentation, affordable compute, and room for independent research.

### What matters to us

- **Freedom to build.** Open code, shared methods, and clear licenses that let people study, adapt, and redistribute the work. Be explicit about what is open—and what isn't.
- **Practical access.** Efficient tools and models that make better use of everyday hardware. Cost and setup effort matter alongside capability.
- **Local control and portability.** Favor systems people can run themselves, connect to other tools, and keep using when a service changes or disappears.
- **Honest, shared research.** Reproducible experiments, useful documentation, and limitations reported as clearly as successes. Small findings and negative results count.

### Start with useful work

Our first priority is practical open-source tooling: small enough to maintain, useful on its own, and easy for others to build upon. We want to earn trust through working software and shared results before making bigger promises.

CommonFIRE is just getting started. Initial projects, governance, and funding are still being shaped; we are not claiming an established research lab, compute service, or grant program.

### There is more than one way to contribute

Writing code is one route. Testing on modest hardware, improving installation guides, documenting a limitation, or sharing a reproducible experiment can be just as valuable. As projects take shape, their repositories will be the place to report problems and contribute.

The aim is a future for AI shaped by many people—not controlled by any single company or government.
